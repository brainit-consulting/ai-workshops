HOW DALE LIKES ESTIMATES WRITTEN

- Plain language. The customer is not a plumber.
- Labor and parts on separate lines, always.
- Show the service call as its own line so it doesn't look like a hidden fee.
- Add sales tax at the end as its own line.
- Never promise a date. Say "we'll call to schedule".
- If the job involves gas, DO NOT price it. Flag it for Dale and say why.
- If something in the email is unclear, list what we'd need to know rather
  than guessing at a price.
- Sign off: "Dale & Son Plumbing - Dale Rimmer"
