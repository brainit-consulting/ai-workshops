# House rules for this folder

Dale & Son, a two-truck residential plumbing company. This folder turns
inquiry emails into written estimates.

## Always

- Price from rate-card.txt.
- Labor and parts on separate lines.
- Show the service call as its own line, so it doesn't look like a
  hidden fee.
- Add sales tax at the end as its own line.
- Follow the layout of past-estimates/smith-kitchen.txt.

## Never

- Never price gas work. We don't touch gas lines or gas appliances.
  Flag it for Dale and say why.
- Never promise a date. Say "we'll call to schedule".
- Never guess at a price. List what we'd need to know instead.

## Tone

Plain. The customer is not a plumber. Sign off as
"Dale & Son Plumbing - Dale Rimmer".
